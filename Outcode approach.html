HO.ME – Hospital Near Me (Neonatal)

Identify the local neonatal unit for a baby based on postcode or hospital.
Data source: London Neonatal Networks & HO.ME tool (Sept 2025).

London Neonatal & Maternity Catchment Map

⚠️ **Important Difference between Search and Map**
The search box above uses the **outward code** (first half of postcode) which may show **multiple** local units.
The interactive map (via the link below) allows you to enter the **full postcode** for a more precise, single catchment area match.

View London Maternity & Neonatal Catchment Map (Interactive)

**Map Instructions:**
1. **Search**: Enter the full postcode (e.g., *SE1 7EH*) into the map's search box and press Enter.
2. **Clear Search**: Delete the postcode from the map's search box to view the coloured area beneath the result.
3. **Tap Area**: Tap the coloured region to see a pop-up with the name of the responsible hospital.

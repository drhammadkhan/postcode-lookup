Privacy

Privacy and data use

HO.ME is designed to perform postcode lookups in the browser and collect only limited, aggregate information about searches.

Postcode lookups

The postcode lookup dataset is downloaded to your browser. A postcode entered into the search box is matched against that local dataset; the full postcode is not sent to the search-statistics service.

After a successful search, the postcode is added to the page address so the result can be bookmarked or shared. If that address is shared or the page is reloaded, the full address, including its postcode parameter, may be included in standard web-hosting request logs.

Aggregate search statistics

When a syntactically valid postcode is searched, HO.ME derives its postcode sector, such as SE1 7, and records the sector together with whether the postcode was found. This allows aggregate demand and out-of-area searches to be counted without storing the full postcode.

The tracking store contains counters by postcode sector and outcome. It does not contain names, user accounts, patient records or full postcodes.

Technical providers

The site is delivered using GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. Mapping resources are loaded from unpkg and OpenFreeMap. Like most web services, these providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser details and requested URLs to deliver and protect their services.

Cookies and advertising

HO.ME does not set first-party cookies, create advertising profiles or include advertising trackers.

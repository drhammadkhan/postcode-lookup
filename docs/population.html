Live Births by Catchment

ONS live birth registrations 2016–2018 (combined 3-year total) allocated to each hospital’s catchment area by care level.

Methodology — proportional sector splitting: Birth data is published at postcode-sector level (e.g. SW1A 1). Because a single sector can span multiple hospital catchments, births are split in proportion to the share of full postcodes within each sector assigned to each hospital. For example, if 70% of postcodes in a sector route to Hospital A and 30% to Hospital B, Hospital A receives 70% of that sector’s births.

About HO.ME

HO.ME is a browser-based postcode lookup for neonatal hospital units in and around London.

What the lookup provides

For a London-area postcode, HO.ME identifies the closest hospital with a neonatal unit at any level and the closest units providing Level 1 special care, Level 2 high dependency care and Level 3 neonatal intensive care. Results include straight-line distances and an interactive map.

The hospital directory also shows the units included in the lookup, their neonatal care level, network, contact number and listed specialties.

How results are produced

The lookup covers approximately 333,000 London-area postcodes derived from the May 2026 Office for National Statistics Postcode Directory. It compares postcode and hospital coordinates using geographic distance calculations and applies the project’s north and south Thames routing rules.

The postcode and hospital datasets are prepared in advance and downloaded as static files. Searches are matched within the browser rather than sent to a remote postcode search service.

Use as a guide

The closest hospital is not necessarily the local hospital or the correct clinical destination. Confirm local referral pathways, the required level of neonatal care and current service arrangements before making a referral or transfer decision. HO.ME does not provide emergency medical advice.
